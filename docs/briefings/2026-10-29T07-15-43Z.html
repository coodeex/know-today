👀 Keep an eye on

  • Curated sets of accepted work can become reusable AI skills that enforce a recognizable output style across multi-step production.
  • Screenshots, word-level transcripts, and audio waveforms give agents concrete checks for visual layout and media timing.
  • Coding agents are becoming a control plane for browser actions, asset retrieval, generative media, and 3D tooling in one workflow.
  • Structured subagents with roles, logs, and final outputs may outperform costly agent teams when coordination adds little decision quality.
  • Automated publishing systems need a final truth check because external claims can change between drafting and distribution.

🚀 Build something ambitious

Outcome-led media operating system

Build a system that turns a company’s product knowledge and market signals into a continuous, measurable distribution engine: it researches claims, creates branded multimedia, verifies time-sensitive facts, publishes across channels, handles qualified inbound interest, and learns from performance. Connect a coding-agent runtime, browser automation, transcription, asset libraries, generative media, analytics, CRM, and publishing APIs; give the system authority over scheduled content and escalation rules for disputed claims, sensitive replies, and commercial commitments. The first meaningful milestone is a weekly production cycle that produces approved brand-consistent assets and shows which formats drive qualified conversations.

Reference-trained creative production platform

Turn a team’s best campaigns, demos, and explainers into a compounding creative system that can generate new work in the same operating style. The workflow ingests accepted outputs, extracts pacing and layout patterns, stores reusable assets and constraints, generates media, renders artifacts, inspects screenshots and timing, and scores each output against a maintained evaluation set. Connect multimodal models, transcription, rendering tools, asset management, and an evaluation layer; escalate only when quality signals conflict or rights and claim checks require judgment. The first meaningful milestone is a repeatable short-form video or product-demo pipeline that matches a defined house style across ten new assets.

Decision council with an evidence trail

Create an internal decision system that assigns bounded perspectives to subagents, gathers positions sequentially, records disagreements, produces a recommendation, and preserves the full reasoning trail for later outcome analysis. Connect durable role instructions, internal data, document retrieval, structured debate, scoring, and reporting; authorize it to prepare decisions and trigger data collection while escalating material commitments, weak evidence, and conflicting policy constraints. The first meaningful milestone is a monthly product-prioritization cycle with auditable recommendations and a scorecard comparing predicted versus actual outcomes.

⚡ Fast builds to try

  • Screenshot QA bot: capture staged screenshots of a web flow, ask an AI model to flag spacing and hierarchy defects, and get a concise visual bug list.
  • Transcript cut checker: align a spoken clip with word timestamps before editing, and get warnings for cuts that clip a word or pause awkwardly.
  • Golden-set evaluator: collect twenty accepted examples, write five observable quality rules, and get a reusable scorecard for new outputs.
  • Topic collision detector: log published themes in a small database, compare each draft against them, and get a duplication warning before scheduling.

⚠️ Caveats

  • Verify reported results: productivity, quality, cost, growth, and runtime claims are anecdotal and need independent measurement.
  • Validate integrations: browser, media, 3D, and publishing workflows depend on credentials, local setup, external services, and platform rules.
  • Test the objective: AI judges and self-improving loops can reinforce weak metrics or misleading acceptance criteria.
  • Check obligations: automated publication, downloaded media, engagement automation, and commercial handling require IP, privacy, disclosure, and platform-policy review.

🧠 The author's perspective

The author believes that today’s movement toward persistent skills, reference-trained evaluations, visual artifact checks, and connected coding-agent workflows aligns with the view that software development and company operations are becoming increasingly autonomous. The emerging pattern is not an isolated chat task but a capable runtime coordinating specialized tools, durable instructions, operational data, and scoped authority across planning, production, testing, publication, and learning from outcomes. Structured subagents, audit trails, performance feedback, and explicit escalation conditions support the author’s expectation that people will build the harness, encode guardrails and reusable learnings, and complete the real-world work agents delegate while autonomous systems coordinate increasingly complete company loops.
