👀 Keep an eye on

  • Aegis Rider Vision anchors navigation and vehicle information relative to the motorcycle as the rider moves their head.
  • Its tracking design combines a helmet-mounted camera and inertial sensors with motorcycle position data from a handlebar-mounted phone or optional Pro-Box.
  • The system combines turn guidance, speed information and a front-facing action camera in one helmet.

Reflection: Interview premium motorcycle owners. Compare willingness to pay against other upgrades.

🚀 Build something ambitious

A spatial navigation operating system for motorcycle fleets

Help touring operators deliver consistent routes and useful trip records across groups. Connect route planning, live location, spatial display hardware and ride recordings; use AI to adapt itineraries to changing conditions and turn completed trips into searchable operator knowledge.

Give the system authority to propose and distribute route changes within operator-defined constraints; compare recommendations with actual trip outcomes, and escalate uncertain road conditions or safety-critical decisions to an operator. The first milestone is a complete guided group trip with route updates and a post-ride summary, evaluated against rider and operator feedback.

⚡ Fast builds to try

  • Helmet display mockup: Ask an AI design tool to place speed and a turn arrow on a road image; get a quick visual for discussing information placement.
  • Premium accessory comparison: Give an AI assistant a handful of public motorcycle accessory listings and ask it to extract prices and features; get a compact starting point for customer interviews.

⚠️ Caveats

Safety: The reported display behavior does not establish that it reduces distraction or performs reliably in real riding. Price, availability and recording specifications should be checked with the manufacturer; reported prices vary, and the storage-duration claim was not independently verified.
