👀 Keep an eye on

  • AI products are shifting from chat surfaces into operating layers that combine persistent context, tools, event hooks, interfaces, and feedback.
  • Structured decision outputs such as predicates, choices, scores, and confidence can make model calls dependable workflow-control components rather than prose generators.
  • Model routing makes cheap, fast models practical for repetitive sub-agents while stronger models handle complex implementation and computer use.
  • Interactive generated UI, dashboards, and visual explainers could turn a conversation into a usable decision and execution surface.
  • Specialist-agent orchestration is becoming a more credible product architecture than one general-purpose assistant with unrestricted authority.

🧭 What changed

  • Reported workspace features bring document, slide, design, and cross-conversation context work closer to the agent interface.
  • Reported computer-use agents can act through browser and software interfaces, pushing permission design and escalation conditions into the core product architecture.
  • Large open-weight releases broaden self-hosted options for organisations with serious serving infrastructure.
  • Local meeting transcription points toward useful privacy-preserving work capture where cloud recording is unsuitable.

Verification note: several named releases, prices, capabilities, availability claims, and benchmarks are incomplete or promotional claims; verify product terms, access, data handling, and performance on your workload.

💭 Opinions worth testing

  • Opinion: The workflow harness matters more than the chosen foundation model for most operational outcomes.
  • Opinion: Most organisations should shape vendor models with domain context, tools, and interfaces rather than attempt to build foundation models.
  • Opinion: Narrow, evaluable domains produce more useful AI systems than all-purpose assistant scope.
  • Opinion: Custom tools can outperform broad enterprise products for a specific team when they remove workflow friction.
  • Opinion: Existing coding-agent capabilities should be extended with a domain layer instead of rebuilt from direct model calls.
  • Opinion: Interface design is a neglected layer that can determine whether comparable AI capability becomes adopted work.
  • Opinion: Interactive generated UI is more consequential than incremental coder features because it changes the shape of conversational software.
  • Opinion: Premium inference speed earns its cost only where elapsed interaction time has direct economic value.
  • Opinion: Open-weight models at extreme scale are enterprise infrastructure choices, not developer-laptop choices.

Reflection: Map a high-frequency workflow. Encode its context and acceptance criteria. Route tasks by complexity. Measure cycle time and rework. Design the interface around the decision being made.

🔮 Predictions to track

  • Prediction: Durable advantage will move toward curated data, task decomposition, organisational adaptation, and harness design as models become less differentiated.
  • Prediction: Individual local workflows will mature into shared team systems once their context, skills, and evaluation loops are made durable.
  • Prediction: Personal-agent products will converge around computer use, task execution, and orchestration, making integration, trust, and economics the lasting differentiators.
  • Prediction: Announcement cadence across major AI vendors will keep product selection fluid, favouring portable integrations over permanent platform commitments.

Reflection: Keep integrations portable. Separate policy from model choice. Preserve structured task data. Benchmark capability changes quarterly.

🚀 Build something ambitious

Operational intelligence harness for a revenue or delivery team

Outcome: Turn scattered internal knowledge into faster, evidence-bound decisions and continuously improve the organisation’s operating memory.

Workflow: Sync approved documents and systems into a governed knowledge layer; answer only from that corpus; turn unanswered questions into coverage gaps; route requests to research, document, analysis, and implementation specialists; capture outcome data to improve skills and source quality.

Connected capabilities: Retrieval, MCP-style system connections, structured-decision calls, reusable skills, event hooks, specialist sub-agents, dashboard generation, and confidence-based human escalation for consequential actions.

First meaningful milestone: Ship one team workflow that answers a recurring operational question with linked evidence, creates an assigned follow-up when evidence is missing, and reports weekly coverage and resolution quality.

Autonomous product-delivery control plane

Outcome: Convert customer signals and product work into a coordinated loop that prioritises, implements, tests, and prepares releases with clear authority boundaries.

Workflow: Ingest feedback and support signals; classify and score opportunities; have a planning agent form implementation packets; delegate coding, testing, and review to specialised agents; run checks through hooks; assemble a release brief and request human help only for defined external or material decisions.

Connected capabilities: Cheap model routing for classification, strong coding agents for implementation, structured decisions for triage, computer use for scoped software tasks, durable instructions, evaluation metrics, and product analytics feedback.

First meaningful milestone: Close the loop from a tagged customer issue to a tested pull request and an evidence-backed release recommendation while tracking time-to-resolution and post-release impact.

Privacy-first meeting-to-execution operating system

Outcome: Make meetings produce accountable work without making sensitive discussion a permanent cloud data exhaust.

Workflow: Transcribe locally where appropriate; extract decisions, commitments, and risks into structured records; route each commitment to a specialist workflow; generate dashboards of ownership and blocked work; feed completion outcomes back into meeting templates and team skills.

Connected capabilities: Local transcription, structured extraction, confidence scoring, document generation, task-system integration, dashboards, and permission-aware automation.

First meaningful milestone: Run a complete weekly leadership cadence where every commitment has an owner, deadline, status signal, and automatically prepared next-meeting agenda.

⚡ Fast builds to try

  • Decision router: Define five support or product categories as fixed outputs and test a structured model call against 30 real examples; get a measurable routing baseline today.
  • Post-edit verification hook: Add a test command that runs after agent-authored changes in one repository; get immediate evidence on whether the coding workflow preserves build health.
  • Interactive brief generator: Ask an AI workspace to turn a current planning question into a form, calculator, and chart; get a decision-ready interface without a separate front-end sprint.
  • Knowledge-gap ledger: Log every internal question that cannot be answered from approved context; get a ranked backlog for documentation and data-connection work.
  • Latency value test: Time one interactive editing or browser workflow at standard and premium speed; get a concrete threshold for when faster inference is worth paying for.

🧠 The author's perspective

The author believes that today’s movement toward durable context, reusable skills, tool connections, structured decisions, computer use, specialist delegation, and event-driven verification strongly aligns with the view that software development and company operations are becoming increasingly autonomous through capable runtimes rather than isolated chat tasks. The emerging harness pattern maps directly to planning, implementation, testing, review, and deployment loops; model routing and specialist agents support coordinated groups of sub-agents; and bounded knowledge systems, permission-aware integrations, confidence thresholds, and outcome feedback provide the practical conditions for agents to retain useful skills, make data-driven decisions, request human help when real-world interaction is needed, and operate end-to-end systems with appropriate authority.
